## Source observations from inspection (distinguish from executed tests)

- The current OCR matcher accepts text fragments anywhere in one crop and sorts up to three fuzzy matches **by match confidence**, **not by their physical left/center/right card positions**. This cannot safely assign a score to a card.
- The automatic reroll detector hashes the crop, returns `unchanged` when full-frame hash distance does not exceed the threshold, and can preserve previous OCR results after an unmatched reroll.
- A zero-contribution augment leads to the observed 2.1/10 default formula; no performance evidence is required for this artifact. A threshold below 4 may wrongly advocate reroll in this scenario. Differentiate *unknown data* from *bad fit*.
- The client does not reset champion/time/gold header on `no_game` or `game_ended`; after game end the server goes through a postgame window. These are code findings explaining plausible observed stale values, not a full device diagnosis.
- Initial custom-match detection started working later in the same test; do **not** assert customs are unsupported. Tesseract green only indicates the executable was found, not correct 3-card OCR.
- Respect Riot restrictions, player-visible data only, no auto-select/buy, no claims of policy approval for experimental statistical augment scores. Do not copy protected source/assets from competitors.
