# Riot policy quotes (read 2026-11-20)
Sources: https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol , https://developer.riotgames.com/policies/general (last updated 2025-05-29).
Excerpts (verbatim fragments):
- [lol, Unapproved Use Cases] "Products cannot display win rates for Augments or Arena Mode items." / "This applies to all websites, applications and overlays."
- [lol, Unapproved Use Cases] "Products may not provide any game-session-specific information that would be previously unknown to the player."
- [lol, Unapproved Use Cases] "Apps that dictate player decisions."
- [lol, Approved (personal keys)] "Game overlays that provide static data that is available prior to the game."
- [lol, Game Integrity] "Products should not remove game decisions, but may highlight decisions that are important"
- [lol, Game Integrity] "Products must not use or incorporate information not present in the game client that would give players a competitive edge" ... "especially when such data is not already accessible through regular gameplay."
- [general, Game Integrity] "Products cannot create an unfair advantage for players, like a cheating program"
- [general, Product Registration] "All products must be registered in, and audited by Riot Games through the Developer Portal" / "If your product utilizes the League Client API" -> register.
- [lol, Registration] "If your product serves players, you must register it with us" ... "regardless of whether or not your product uses official documented APIs."
- [lol, Game Client API, paraphrase not verbatim] LCU "not officially supported for use with third party applications".
Not found on either page: Mayhem, OCR/screen reading, click/hotkey monitoring, augment recommendations (explicitly). Those remain [Por validar] -> registration/audit or ask Riot.
